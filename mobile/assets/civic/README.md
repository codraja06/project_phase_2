# CivicTrack reference photography

These are licensed reference photographs, locally bundled for predictable loading.
They are not complaint records, AI evidence, official municipal assets or endorsements.

Full attribution and source/license links are in `credits.json` and in the expandable
**Photography & visual identity** section in Citizen and Officer Profile screens.
Images were resized and JPEG compressed; display may crop them and add a dark green tint.
Adapted CC BY-SA photographs remain available under the same license listed for each
image. This attribution applies to the photograph and its adaptation, not the app code.
